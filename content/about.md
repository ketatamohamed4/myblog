## Who I am

I'm Mohamed Ketata, a 2nd year student at ENIS, National Engineering School Of Sfax Tunisia, focused on cloud infrastructure and DevOps. I learn by building things, breaking them, and writing down how I fixed them. This blog is where I document that process, including the errors, the dead ends, and the reasoning that got me unstuck.

## What I've worked on

**Virtual Desktop Infrastructure (VDI) at Safozi.** I started as an intern at Safozi Cloud Infrastructure, then continued as a part-time team member. My project was commercializing an open-source VDI architecture. That meant researching platforms, building proofs of concept, and comparing them on both technical and business criteria. Along the way I worked with:

- **Proxmox VE** for local virtualization experiments
- **Ubuntu Server** on repurposed hardware
- **Tailscale** for secure remote SSH access
- **IsardVDI** and **ABCDesktop** as open-source VDI and remote-desktop-as-a-service candidates

I'm writing this up as a four-part series, including the hardware limits I hit and the final comparison.

**Computer vision.** A Python project using MediaPipe that reads American Sign Language letters from a live camera feed and triggers an operating system action for each one. For example, signing "S" opens Spotify.

**Mobile development.** A carpooling (covoiturage) app for Tunisia, built with Node.js.

**Embedded systems and IoT.** SALTEK, a low-cost (under $100), autonomous, solar-powered irrigation system designed to help smallholder farmers manage water salinity.

## What I'm learning now

I'm working through the [LearnToCloud](https://learntocloud.guide/) guide to build a structured foundation in cloud computing and DevOps. I'll publish my notes and small projects here as I go.
